# Loorio pitch — edit this file; the AI reads it fresh for every draft

## What Loorio is
Loorio is a new short-form video app for college students: the scroll of TikTok/Reels, but every
video teaches you something. Study tips, SAT/ACT strategy, money, science, history, skills — quick,
genuinely useful, and not "academic". We're pre-launch and building our founding creator roster.

## What we're asking creators for
Join Loorio as a founding creator: cross-post the educational short-form videos they already make
(no new content required). We handle the upload if they want.

## What's in it for them
- Founding-creator badge and front-of-feed placement at launch
- A new audience of students actively choosing to learn, not just scroll
- Direct line to the founder; input on creator features before anyone else

## Tone
Founder-to-creator, casual and specific. Short (under 120 words for the first email). Reference one
real thing about their content. No hype words, no "I hope this email finds you well", no
attachments, one clear ask: "open to a quick yes/no?" or "want me to send details?"

## Facts the AI must not invent
Don't promise money, payouts, revenue share, follower counts, launch dates or partnerships unless
they're written in this file.
